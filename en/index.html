Tour itinerary

We head out to a shifting dunes park towards our first stop:

  1. Parnamirim LagoonThe traditional lagoon of sand sledding (skibunda) and water slide, one of Cumbuco's most famous spots.
  2. Shifting Dunes ParkA thrilling ride across the dunes, “with thrills” or “smooth ride”. Photo stop at the famous Duna do Jegue viewpoint (photos included).
  3. Banana LagoonLeisure time with optional activities: Zipline · Banana Boat · Crazy Tube · Jet ski · Speedboat · Soap football · Catapult.
  4. Second Shifting Dunes ParkThe adventure continues across the dunes towards the Cauípe River, through stunning natural scenery.
  5. Cauípe River MouthOur final stop is one of the most special settings of the tour: where the river meets the sea, surrounded by nature and beautiful scenery.
  6. ReturnThen we head back.

Note: activities are optional and charged separately.

Your safety

Licensed Buggy

An identified, documented vehicle authorised to run tourist tours in Cumbuco.

How to identify a licensed buggy?

Before boarding, check that the buggy is properly authorised to run tourist tours in Cumbuco.

  • Red licence plate

    Shows the vehicle is authorised for paid passenger transport, just like taxis.

  • Identification number

    Licensed buggies carry their own number, from 01 to 189, matching the vehicles licensed in the municipality of Caucaia.

  • Authorisation permit

    The driver must carry a permit issued by the City Transport Department, identifying and authorising both vehicle and professional to run buggy tours in Cumbuco.

  • Safety and peace of mind

    By choosing a licensed driver, you can check the vehicle's documents and identification before the tour starts.
